When a military-grade version of Starlink relied on by Ukraine was throttled on Oct. 14, it sparked panic in Kyiv and confusion inside the Trump administration over who ordered the restrictions.

Our reporting shows Elon Musk authorized the Starlink slowdown .

The SpaceX founder has been discussing Ukraine with President Trump, but the president has denied ordering the restrictions.

Volodymyr Zelensky's complaints—namely that Ukrainians could die if the unrestrictive service wasn't restored—led to an informal investigation inside the White House.

The episode has spiked U.S.-Ukraine tensions to their highest level since Trump berated Zelensky in the Oval Office last year. But it has also highlighted Musk's singular influence over the war. With a seeming flick of a switch, he has the power to alter the course of a deadly conflict.

4. READ IT HERE FIRST

Autism-therapy workers say big chains pressured them to boost treatment hours.

Medicaid plans and private insurers will often pay for up to about 40 hours of autism therapy a week. At some large chains backed by private-equity-investors, former employees told the Journal they felt pressure to maximize therapy hours , claims that the companies dispute. Our reporters analyzed the billings of five of the largest private equity-backed providers of autism therapy to Medicaid patients. Together, the group's average weekly therapy hours for those patients were 29% higher than competitors' in 2024. All but one of those companies delivered more hours than local rivals, report Anna Wilde Mathews, Christopher Weaver and Tom McGinty. Medicaid programs financed by state and federal taxpayers paid $10 billion for autism therapy last year, up from around $2 billion in 2021, driven in part by increasing treatment hours per patient.

NYSE is getting trounced in the battle for Wall Street's biggest IPOs.

The New York Stock Exchange and Nasdaq have spent years trying to outdo each other as they compete for the hottest new listings, pitching companies on their ability to generate buzz and offering goodies such as billboards and lavish galas. Lately, it's not much of a fight . The Nasdaq has won the listings of 59 companies raising nearly $120 billion in traditional IPOs so far this year through Friday, according to Dealogic, while the NYSE has landed 32 companies raising $20 billion. Some NYSE officials privately grouse that a decision by Nasdaq to fast-track the addition of massive newly public companies like SpaceX to its popular Nasdaq-100 index made it all but impossible to compete. The Texas Stock Exchange is also an emerging threat, reports Corrie Driebusch.

If you want to make a friend, tell the whole world you're looking for one.

Loneliness used to be a shameful secret. Now, people are confessing their isolation to thousands of strangers on TikTok and Reddit hoping one of them might want to hang out. The classifieds-like posts open candidly: "This is going to seem sad, but I really need to find some friends" or "I'm tired of being lonely." Some draw internet trolls but far more garner dozens of supportive replies . And a surprising number yield real-world best friends.
